Welcome to SOY Riverview

Located in Soy, Uasin Gishu, SOY Riverview Resort provides a peaceful escape with scenic nature views, hiking trails, and the finest dining experience in the region.

Our mission: To offer world-class hospitality while preserving the natural beauty of the riverside. Every stay is crafted to rejuvenate your soul.
